## Phenix Rods — Upgrade Fee Formula

**Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of new rod) − (MSRP of broken rod) + (tier fee of broken rod)**

*Example: Feather FTX-C71MH → Ultra MBX UMBX-C700MH*
$329.99 − $169.99 + $55.00 = **$215.00** (plus return shipping and applicable taxes)

## Phenix Axis Rod — Tier Fee by Action

**Is there a difference in replacement fee between the Axis ML model and the Axis X4H model?**
**What is the Axis replacement fee?**
Yes — significant difference. The Axis series has two tiers depending on rod action: **Axis ML–XH action = Tier-3 ($95 replacement fee)**; **Axis X2H–X4H action = Tier-4 ($125 replacement fee)**. Always check the action designation on your rod blank before calculating the replacement cost. The Axis X2H, X3H, and X4H models cost $125 to replace, not $95.

## Phenix Abyss HD — Tier-3 Clarification

**What tier is the Abyss HD?**
**How much does it cost to replace a Phenix Abyss HD (8'2")?**
The Abyss HD is always **Tier-3 ($95 replacement fee)**, regardless of rod length. Do NOT confuse with the regular Abyss series: regular Abyss 9' and under = Tier-2 ($70); regular Abyss 10' and above = Tier-3 ($95). The Abyss HD is a separate premium series — always Tier-3. For an 8'2" Abyss HD: $95 tier fee + $50 return shipping (over 7'11") = **$145 total** before tax.

## Phenix Rods — Tier Fees Questions

**How much is return shipping?**
$20 for rods 7'10" and under. $50 for rods 7'11" and over. Custom quote for outside continental US — email fishinginfo@gsmorg.com before shipping.

**How much does it cost to replace a Crankbait XG?**
**What is the total cost to replace a Crankbait XG 7'11" trolling model?**
The Crankbait XG is Tier-1 ($55 replacement fee). Most XG models — 7', 7'4", and 7'9" — are 7'10" and under, so return shipping is $20, making the total **$75**. However, the XG4-T 7'11" trolling model is 7'11" and over, so return shipping is $50, making the total **$105**. Always confirm your model's length before quoting.

**How is the upgrade fee calculated?**
Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of new rod) − (MSRP of broken rod) + (tier fee of broken rod). Example: Feather ($169.99) upgraded to Ultra MBX ($329.99) = $329.99 − $169.99 + $55.00 = $215.00 upgrade fee, plus return shipping.

**Is the blank/section fee different from the complete rod fee?**
Yes — blanks and sections have a separate lower tier fee table. A Feather blank is $40 (Tier-1 blank) vs $55 (Tier-1 complete rod). See the Blanks & Sections table above.

**What is the minimum total cost for any Phenix replacement?**
**What is the cheapest Phenix rod replacement?**
Minimum total = **$75**: Tier-1 replacement fee ($55) + $20 return shipping (rod 7'10" or under). Do NOT quote $55 alone — that is only the tier fee, not the total. The $20 return shipping always applies.
Exactly 3 rod series are Tier-1 ($55 replacement fee — the cheapest tier):
1. Crankbait XG
2. Feather (bass series — NOT the Iron Feather trout series which is Tier-5)
3. Maxim
All three cost the same. Always list all three when answering cheapest-replacement questions.
