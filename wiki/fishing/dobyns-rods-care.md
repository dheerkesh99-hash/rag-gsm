## Dobyns Rods - Rod Care & Maintenance - Overview

Proper care and maintenance will help keep your Dobyns rod in top condition for many seasons. This page covers guide cleaning, blank and reel seat cleaning, cork handle care, rod storage, preventive damage tips, and how to request free replacement parts (tips and guides).

For warranty coverage, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]]. For the replacement process if your rod breaks, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]].

---

## Dobyns Rods - Guide Cleaning Instructions

- Mix dish soap with water and use a cotton swab to lightly clean around the guide ring and guide foot area.
- Rinse with clean water and dry with a microfiber towel.
- If using the rod in saltwater, apply WD-40 on the guides after cleaning.

Clean guides after every use, especially after saltwater exposure, to prevent corrosion and buildup that could damage your line.

---

## Dobyns Rods - Blank and Reel Seat Cleaning Instructions

- Use either dish soap and water, or isopropyl rubbing alcohol on a clean towel.
- Rinse with clean water and dry with a microfiber towel.
- Spray the blank from above the reel seat to the tip with Pledge and wipe off with a microfiber towel.

This removes salt residue, dirt, and grime and helps maintain the blank's finish.

## Dobyns Rods - Cork Handle Care Instructions

Extend the life of your cork handle by applying a product like **U-40 Cork Seal**. A few light coats of cork sealer make cleaning easier and prolong the life of the cork.

If the cork shows wear from prior use, clean it with a **Magic Eraser** and allow it to dry before applying cork sealer. If the cork begins to show pitting, fill the voids with a **color-matching wood filler**, allow it to dry, and sand it flush with the handle using **400-grit sandpaper**. Clean the cork dust off with dish soap, water, and a clean towel, allow it to fully dry, and then apply a light coat of cork sealer.

## Dobyns Rods - Rod Storage Best Practices

- Store your rods in a **mesh or neoprene rod sleeve** when not in use.
- Store rods in a **temperature-controlled environment** in a **vertical position** with the tips not leaning against a wall.
- A good **rod rack** is recommended for storage.
- Rods leaned against a wall can develop memory over time and weaken the blank.

Avoid storing rods in extreme heat or cold (e.g., inside a vehicle trunk in summer).

---

## Dobyns Rods - Preventive Tips to Avoid Damage

- Always clean your rod after each use, especially after saltwater exposure.
- Avoid storing rods in extreme heat or cold (e.g., inside a vehicle trunk in summer).
- Use a padded rod sleeve during transport to prevent accidental breakage.
- Do not lean rods against walls for extended periods. Use a vertical rod rack instead.
- Inspect guides regularly for cracks or chips that could damage your line.
- Apply cork sealer periodically to prevent cork degradation over time.

**How should I clean Dobyns rod guides?**
Clean the guides with dish soap, water, and a cotton swab. Rinse and dry with a microfiber towel.
