## Phenix Rods — Pricing Questions

**How much does the Iron Feather cost?**
Complete spinning rods: $549.99–$579.99. Blanks: $249.99–$269.99. It is the highest-priced trout/ultralight rod in the Phenix lineup.

**How much does the M1 Bass cost?**
Complete rods: $99.99–$219.99 depending on length and action. One of the best value premium bass rods in the lineup.

**How much does the Trifecta cost?**
Trifecta (original): $189.99–$279.99. Trifecta Pro and Trifecta Lite: $57.50–$279.99 (low end of range includes blanks).

**How much does the Cicada cost?**
Complete casting and spinning rods: $279.99–$339.99. Blanks start from $70.00.

**How much does the Abyss HD cost?**
Complete rods: $229.99–$329.99. Blanks: $55.00–$99.99.

**How much does the Feather cost?**
Complete rods: $169.99–$379.99. Blanks: $89.99–$169.99.

**What is the most affordable Phenix rod series?**
Freshwater: Maxim ($129.99–$149.99) and Crankbait XG ($159.99–$199.99). Saltwater: RTS Inshore ($199.99–$229.99). Salmon/Steelhead: Trifecta ($189.99–$279.99).

**What is the most affordable bass rod in the Phenix lineup?**
The Maxim ($129.99–$149.99) is Phenix's purpose-built value bass rod and the most affordable bass series in the lineup. Note: while the M1 Bass series has models starting at $99.99, the M1 Bass is a tournament-grade performance rod — the Maxim is the entry-level value option. The Maxim uses 30-ton Multi-Axis Carbon Fiber with K-Woven outer scrim and camo EVA grips.

**What is the most premium Phenix rod?**
Freshwater: K2 ($559.99–$649.99). Saltwater: Axis ($259.99–$539.99) and Black Diamond Hybrid ($257.40–$589.99). Trout: Iron Feather ($549.99–$579.99).

**Why do some series show such a wide price range?**
Each series has multiple models (different lengths and line weights) and sub-types (casting vs spinning, blanks vs complete rods). The range shown covers the lowest-priced to highest-priced product in the series. Check phenixrods.com for per-model pricing.
