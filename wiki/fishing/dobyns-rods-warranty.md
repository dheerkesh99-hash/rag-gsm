## Dobyns Rods — Warranty — Overview

Dobyns Rods offers warranty protection on all rod series against defects in materials and workmanship. Coverage is either a **Limited Lifetime Warranty** or a **1-Year Warranty** depending on the rod series. All warranties apply to the **original owner only**.

This page covers warranty coverage, eligibility, the manufacturing defect claim process, and the Maverick Reel warranty. For the No-Hassle Replacement process (accidental breakage), see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]]. For tier fees and shipping costs, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]].

---

## Dobyns Rods — Warranty — Service Paths

Dobyns provides three main service paths depending on the situation:

- **Manufacturing Defect Claim** — For rods that break within 60 days of purchase. No-cost replacement if a defect is confirmed. Photo inspection only — do not ship the rod.
- **No-Hassle Replacement Program** — For rods broken due to accidents, normal wear and tear, or neglect. A replacement tier fee applies. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]] for the full process.
- **No-Hassle Replacement Warranty Upgrade** — Upgrade a broken rod to a higher series during the replacement process. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]] for details.

## Dobyns Rods — Warranty — What Is Covered

The Dobyns Rods warranty covers:

- **Defects in materials or workmanship** for the lifetime of the original owner (Limited Lifetime series) or one year from the date of purchase (Maverick and Colt).
- **Accidental breakage, normal wear and tear, and neglect** — covered under the No-Hassle Replacement Program, but a tier fee applies. This is not a free replacement. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]] for fee details.

The warranty is limited to the **original owner** only. Proof of purchase from an authorized dealer is required for manufacturing defect claims.

---

## Dobyns Rods — Warranty — What Is Not Covered

- If the exact rod model is no longer available, Dobyns will replace it with the **closest comparable model** from the current product line. Colors and components may vary.
- The No-Hassle Replacement Program requires payment of the applicable **tier fee, return shipping, and sales tax** (where applicable). It does not provide free replacements for non-defective breakage.
- Warranty coverage (free defect replacement) does not transfer to second owners. However, the **No-Hassle Replacement Program is available to anyone**, including second owners — they simply pay the applicable tier fee.

## Dobyns Rods — Manufacturing Defect Claim — Eligibility & Process

If your Dobyns rod breaks within **60 days of purchase**, it may qualify for a **no-cost replacement** if a manufacturing defect is confirmed. Typically, defective rods break within the first few uses.

**Do not ship the rod.** Defect inspections are conducted by **photo only**.

**Email the following to fishinginfo@gsmorg.com:**
1. Multiple clear photos of the point of the break
2. A photo of the Dobyns logo section (directly above the reel seat)
3. A photo of the handle section from the reel seat to the butt of the rod
4. A photo or screenshot of your dated proof of purchase from an authorized dealer
5. Your contact information and full shipping address

You may also call **877-269-8490** for instructions on submitting a defect claim.

---

## Dobyns Rods — Manufacturing Defect Claim — Outcomes

After Dobyns reviews your photos, you will receive one of two outcomes:

- **Defect confirmed:** Rod replaced and shipped at **no cost** to you, including return shipping.
- **Defect not confirmed:** The rod is still eligible for replacement under the **No-Hassle Replacement Program** with applicable tier fees, return shipping, and sales tax. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]] for the process and [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]] for fees.

For background on what causes rod failure, Dobyns recommends the "Rod Failure — Causes & Solutions" article by Tom Kirkman, available at dobynsrods.com.

## Dobyns Rods — Warranty — Questions — Defect Claims

**How do I file a defect claim for my Dobyns rod?**
**My Dobyns rod broke within 60 days — what do I do?**
**My Dobyns Fury broke on the third cast — what should I do?**
**My brand new Dobyns rod broke on the first cast — what do I do?**
