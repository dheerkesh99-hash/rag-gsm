## Bucca Brand — Returns & Refunds — Overview

This page covers the GSM Outdoors return and refund policy as it applies to Bucca Brand products purchased on GSM Outdoors websites (including baits.com). GSM offers free returns for a refund within 30 days of purchase, subject to eligibility requirements.

This page explains eligibility rules, the return process, refund expectations, and important shipping rules. For warranty replacement claims (manufacturing defects), see [[fishing/bucca-brand-warranty]]. For product details, see [[fishing/bucca-brand-product-catalog]].

---

## Bucca Brand — Returns — Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a free return and refund on a Bucca Brand product, all of the following must be met:

1. The order must have been placed on a **GSM Outdoors website** (e.g., baits.com). GSM Outdoors does not offer return refunds on items purchased outside of GSM Outdoors websites.
2. The return must be submitted **within 30 days** of the original purchase date.
3. The product must be **unused and undamaged**.
4. The product must be returned in its **original packaging**.
5. The product must **not be customized**. GSM does not accept returns on customized products.

All five conditions are mandatory. If any condition is not met, the return will not be eligible for a refund.

---

## Bucca Brand — Returns — Authorized Dealer Purchases

**Returns for Bucca Brand products purchased from an authorized dealer (not a GSM Outdoors website) are not handled by GSM Outdoors.** The dealer's own return policy applies. Contact the authorized dealer directly for return and refund information.

GSM Outdoors only processes returns for orders placed on GSM Outdoors websites (such as baits.com).

---

## Bucca Brand — Returns — Refund Process & Timeline

Once your return is received by GSM Outdoors, allow up to **21 business days** to process your refund. Refunds will be returned to the **original form of payment** used for the order.

**Important refund limitations:**
- Any **shipping costs from the original order are non-refundable**.
- Any **additional return shipping costs are non-refundable**.
- GSM Outdoors **does not offer store credit or gift cards** for returned products — refunds go to original payment method only.

---

## Bucca Brand — Returns — Return Authorization (RA) — Rules

**Do NOT ship your product without a Return Authorization.** Any product shipped without a Return Authorization will **not be refunded** and **will be refused** by the GSM warehouse.

After your return request is approved, GSM Outdoors will provide:
- A **Return Authorization (RA) number**
- **Shipping instructions**
- A **pre-paid shipping label**

Only ship your product after you have received all of the above.

---

## Bucca Brand — Returns — Step-by-Step Process

How to return a Bucca Brand product purchased on a GSM Outdoors website:

1. Go to the GSM Outdoors return request page at baits.com/return-request/
2. Submit a return request within **30 days** of your original purchase date
3. Wait for GSM Outdoors to review and approve your request
4. Once approved, you will receive a **Return Authorization (RA) number**, shipping instructions, and a **pre-paid shipping label**
5. Ship the product in its **original packaging**, **unused and undamaged** — do not ship before receiving the RA
6. Once GSM receives your return, allow up to **21 business days** for the refund to be processed to your original payment method
