## E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods — Overview

BoneHead Tackle E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods are designed for sensitivity, responsive action, and dependable durability. The lineup includes multiple rod lengths for anglers targeting different fishing applications. E-Series rods feature stainless steel guides and EVA grips at a more affordable price point than the premium Carbon Fiber series.

## E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods — Specs & Features

**What features do BoneHead E-Series rods have?**
BoneHead E-Series rods feature carbon fiber construction, stainless steel guides, and solid EVA grips.

**What kind of guides do BoneHead E-Series rods use?**
Stainless steel guides.

**What grip do BoneHead E-Series rods have?**
Solid EVA grips.

**Are BoneHead E-Series rods good for lightweight fishing setups?**
Yes. E-Series rods are designed for sensitivity and responsive performance while maintaining durability.

**What sizes are available for BoneHead E-Series rods?**
Available sizes include 12', 10', 8', 7', and 5'8" models.

**What is the shortest BoneHead E-Series rod available?**
The shortest E-Series Carbon Fiber Spinning Rod is the 5'8" model.

**Do you make a 12 foot spinning rod?**
Yes. The E-Series Carbon Fiber Rod is available in a 12 foot model for $79.99. The premium Carbon Fiber line does not include a 12 foot model.
