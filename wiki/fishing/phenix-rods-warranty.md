# Phenix Rods Warranty & Replacement Programs

## Phenix Rods Warranty — Overview

Phenix Rods ("Phenix") warrants all fishing rods and blanks to be free from defects in material and workmanship for the **lifetime of the original owner**. If a rod fails due to a manufacturing defect, Phenix will repair or replace the product at its discretion.

Phenix offers three distinct programs depending on the situation:

1. **America No-Hassle Replacement** — For breaks caused by accidents, normal wear and tear, or neglect. A replacement fee applies.
2. **Claim Manufacturer Defect** — For rods that break within 30 days of purchase. No-cost replacement if a defect is confirmed.
3. **Boron Legacy Program** — For owners of legacy Boron rods from the 1970s–2000s seeking an upgrade.
4. **Replacement fee:** Depends on your rod series — Tier 1 ($55) to Tier 5 ($165) 
plus return shipping ($20 for rods 7'10" and under, $50 for over 7'11"). 
Full fee table: [[fishing/phenix-rods-tier-fees]].
---

## Phenix Rods Warranty — What Is Covered

- Defects in materials or workmanship for the lifetime of the original owner
- Accidental breakage, normal wear and tear, and neglect (under the America No-Hassle Replacement Program, with applicable fees)
- Legacy Boron rods from the 1970s through the 2000s (under the Boron Legacy Program)

## Phenix Rods Warranty — What Is Not Covered

- Fire, intentional breakage, abuse, misuse, or natural disaster
- Unauthorized repair or modification
- Products used for **commercial purposes**
- Incidental or consequential damages of any kind

> **Note:** Colors and components may vary between original and replacement parts. Discontinued models will be replaced with the closest comparable model at the same MSRP.

## Phenix Rods — Program 1 — America No-Hassle Replacement

This is the recommended program for most customers. It covers breaks due to **accidents, normal wear and tear, or neglect** — regardless of cause.

## Phenix No-Hassle Replacement — Options

**Direct Replacement**
- Receive the same rod model in return.
- If the exact model is no longer available, Phenix will substitute the closest comparable model at the same MSRP.
- The logo section of the broken rod must be mailed in.
- Replacement tier fee + return shipping fee + applicable taxes apply.

**Replacement with Upgrade**
- Upgrade to a different model in the same series or a higher series.
- The logo section of the broken rod must be mailed in.
- Upgrade fee + replacement tier fee of the broken rod + return shipping fee + applicable taxes apply.
- No downgrade option is available.

**Upgrade Fee Calculation:**
> Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of new rod) − (MSRP of broken rod) + (Replacement tier fee of broken rod)

*Example: Phenix Feather FTX-C71MH upgraded to Ultra MBX UMBX-C700MH*
> $329.99 (UMBX) − $169.99 (Feather) + $55.00 (Feather tier fee) = **$215.00 upgrade fee**

## Phenix No-Hassle Replacement — How to Submit

1. **Cut the logo section** from the broken rod — a 4"–6" section directly above the reel seat that includes the Phenix logo, rod model number, and specifications. Use a hacksaw, Dremel tool with cut-off wheel, or large garden pruners.
2. **Complete the Warranty Application Form** (page 1). List all broken rod model numbers exactly as printed on the logo section, select casting or spinning, and choose your replacement service (direct or upgrade). Indicate your payment preference.
3. **Pack and ship** the completed form and logo section(s) in a padded shipping envelope **with tracking** to:

   > **Fishing Returns**
   > 5250 Frye Rd
   > Irving, TX 75061

4. **Pay your fee** using one of two options:
   - **Pay by Phone:** Phenix will call you at **877-269-8490** once your package arrives and the return order is ready.
   - **Check or Money Order:** Make payable to **"GSM Outdoors"** for the replacement tier fee, upgrade fees, taxes (if applicable), and return shipping. Include sales tax if shipping to: AR, AZ, CA, GA, TX, ID, IA, LA, MO, MS, OH, UT, KY, MN, NY, TN, WA, or WI.

> ⚠️ Phenix Rods is not responsible for packages lost in transit. Always ship with tracking.

> **Outside the continental U.S.?** Email [fishinginfo@gsmorg.com](mailto:fishinginfo@gsmorg.com) for a custom shipping quote *before* sending your package. Include the rod model you'll receive in return and your full return shipping address.

## Phenix Rods — Program 2 — Manufacturer Defect Claim

If your rod breaks within **30 days of purchase**, it may qualify for a **no-cost replacement** if a manufacturing defect is confirmed.

## Phenix Defect Claim — How to Submit

1. **Do not ship the rod.** Defect inspections are conducted by photo only.
2. **Email the following to [fishinginfo@gsmorg.com](mailto:fishinginfo@gsmorg.com):**
   - Multiple clear photos of the point of the break
   - A photo of the Phenix logo section (directly above the reel seat)
   - A photo of the handle section from the reel seat to the butt of the rod
   - A photo or screenshot of your **dated proof of purchase** from an authorized dealer
   - Your contact information and full shipping address
3. **Await evaluation.** Phenix will review the photos and contact you with a final determination.
   - **Defect confirmed:** Rod replaced and shipped at **no cost**.
   - **Defect not confirmed:** Rod is still eligible for replacement under the America No-Hassle Replacement Program with applicable tier and shipping fees.

## Phenix Boron Legacy — How to Submit

## Phenix Rods Warranty — Frequently Asked Questions

**What programs are available for a broken Phenix rod?**
Phenix offers three programs: (1) the America No-Hassle Replacement for any break with a tier fee, (2) the Manufacturer Defect Claim for breaks within 30 days of purchase that may qualify for no-cost replacement, and (3) the Boron Legacy Program for vintage Boron rod owners.

**How do I pay for my Phenix warranty replacement?**
**What are the payment options for the Phenix No-Hassle Replacement?**
Two payment options: (1) **Pay by Phone** — Phenix will call you at **877-269-8490** once your package arrives and the return order is ready. Pay by credit or debit card at that time. Best option if you are in a sales tax state and unsure of the correct tax amount. (2) **Check or Money Order** — Make payable to "GSM Outdoors" for the tier fee + return shipping + sales tax (if applicable). Include in the same envelope as the logo section and form.

**Do I need to ship my whole rod in?**
For defect inspections (within 30 days of purchase), no — photos via email are required only. For No-Hassle Replacements, you mail only the **logo section** of the broken rod, not the full rod.

**What is the logo section?**
It is the 4"–6" portion of the rod directly above the reel seat, including the Phenix logo, model number decal, and rod specifications printed on the blank. This must accompany your replacement form.

**What if my exact rod model is discontinued?**
Phenix will replace it with the closest comparable model at the same MSRP from the current product line.

**Can I upgrade to a different rod when submitting a replacement?**
Yes. Under the Replacement with Upgrade option, you pay the MSRP difference between the new model and the broken model, plus the broken rod's tier fee, plus return shipping. No downgrading is available.

**Can I process the warranty through a dealer?**
Yes. Customers may also complete the America No-Hassle Replacement Program through a participating Phenix Rods Authorized Dealer.

**Which states require sales tax?**
Sales tax applies when shipping to: AR, AZ, CA, GA, TX, ID, IA, LA, MO, MS, OH, UT, KY, MN, NY, TN, WA, and WI. Add the appropriate tax amount to your check or money order.

**How long does the defect evaluation take?**
The source documents do not specify a turnaround time. Refer customers to [fishinginfo@gsmorg.com](mailto:fishinginfo@gsmorg.com) for current processing timelines.
