## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Overview

> **Note:** This page covers the No-Hassle Replacement Program for broken rods. For free replacement tips and guides (small parts, not broken rods), see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-care]].

The Dobyns Rods No-Hassle Replacement Program covers rod breakage or damage due to **accidents, normal wear and tear, or neglect**. The rod owner pays the applicable replacement tier fee to receive a replacement rod. If the exact model is no longer available, Dobyns will replace it with the closest comparable model from the current product line.

This page covers the step-by-step replacement process, the dealer replacement option, and the warranty upgrade program. For warranty coverage and eligibility, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]]. For tier fees and shipping costs, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]].

---

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - What Is the Logo Section

The **logo section** is the 6-to-8-inch portion of the rod blank directly above the reel seat. It includes the Dobyns logo, rod model number decal, and rod specifications printed on the back side of the blank.

**How to cut it:** Use a hacksaw, Dremel tool with cut-off wheel, or large garden pruners. Cut carefully to preserve the logo, model number, and spec markings. You send only this section, not the full rod.

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Step-by-Step Process

How to submit a No-Hassle Replacement claim:

1. **Cut the logo section** from the broken rod. This is the 6-to-8-inch section directly above the reel seat that includes the Dobyns logo, model number decal, and rod specs.
2. **Download and print** the Dobyns Warranty Information Form (page 1). Fill it out completely: name, phone, email, return shipping address, service requested, and all rod/logo section model numbers.
3. **Pack and ship** the completed form and logo section(s) in a **padded shipping envelope** to: **Fishing Returns, 5250 Frye Rd, Irving, TX 75061**. Send with tracking.
4. **Pay your fee** using one of the payment options described below.
5. Allow up to **2 weeks** for processing after your warranty package is received.

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Payment Options

After your warranty package arrives at the GSM warehouse, you pay the tier fee plus return shipping plus applicable sales tax. Two payment methods are available:

**Option 1 - Pay by Phone (Recommended):** Dobyns will call you once your package arrives and the return order is created. Payment is taken by credit/debit card at **877-269-8490**. This is the best option if you live in a taxable state, as the correct sales tax will be calculated for you.

**Option 2 - Check or Money Order:** Make payable to **"GSM Outdoors"** for the replacement tier fee, return shipping, and applicable taxes. Include it in the shipping envelope with your form and logo section. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]] for the fee table and sales tax states.

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Through a Dealer

If you need a replacement rod immediately, you can take your broken rod to a local **participating Dobyns Rods dealer** and pay the replacement tier fee directly. The dealer will keep your broken rod and provide a new, same-model rod in return.

**Important notes:**
- This depends on the dealer having inventory of your rod model and their willingness to participate.
- Call your local dealer to verify participation and rod availability before visiting.
- Participating dealers **will not upgrade** your broken rod to a higher series. To upgrade, you must send the rod directly to Dobyns.

## Dobyns Rods - Warranty Upgrade - How It Works

If you wish to upgrade your broken rod to a rod in a **higher series**, you can do so through the No-Hassle Replacement process.

**How to submit:**
1. Follow the same steps as the standard No-Hassle Replacement claim.
2. On the Warranty Information Form, list the rod you are sending in on the left and the rod you wish to upgrade to on the right.
3. Send in the **logo section or the whole rod** along with the completed form.

**No downgrade option is available.** You may only upgrade to a rod in a higher series.

## Dobyns Rods - Warranty Upgrade - Fee Calculation

The upgrade fee is calculated as:

**Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of upgrade rod) minus (MSRP of broken rod) + (Broken rod tier fee)**

Return shipping fees and applicable sales taxes also apply.

**Example:** Upgrade a Fury 734C to a Champion Extreme 744C SH. $369.99 (DX MSRP) minus $149.99 (FR MSRP) + $50.00 (FR Tier Fee) = **$270.00 Upgrade Fee**. Plus $30.00 return shipping and applicable sales tax. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-tier-fees]] for the complete fee table.

**My Dobyns rod broke. How do I get a replacement?**
**How do I use the Dobyns No-Hassle Replacement Program?**
Cut the 6-to-8-inch logo section from above the reel seat, fill out the Dobyns Warranty Information Form, and mail both in a padded envelope with tracking to: Fishing Returns, 5250 Frye Rd, Irving, TX 75061. Dobyns will call you for payment (tier fee + $30 return shipping + tax), or you can include a check/money order payable to "GSM Outdoors." Allow up to 2 weeks for processing.

**Do I need to ship my whole rod in?**
**Do I send the entire broken rod?**
No. For No-Hassle Replacements, you mail only the **logo section** (6-to-8-inch section directly above the reel seat), not the full rod. For warranty upgrades, you may send the logo section or the whole rod.

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Questions - Ownership

**Can a second owner use the No-Hassle Replacement Program?**
**My buddy gave me his old Dobyns rod — can I still get a replacement if it breaks?**
**I bought a used Dobyns rod. Am I eligible for No-Hassle Replacement?**
Yes. The No-Hassle Replacement Program is available to **anyone**, including second owners and people who received a rod as a gift. You pay the applicable tier fee and follow the standard replacement process. The only thing that does NOT transfer to a second owner is the free warranty coverage (manufacturing defect claims). Anyone can pay the tier fee and use No-Hassle Replacement.

## Dobyns Rods - No-Hassle Replacement - Questions - Dealer and Upgrade

**Can I process my Dobyns warranty through a dealer?**
**Can I get a replacement rod at a local tackle shop?**
Yes. You can take your broken rod to a participating Dobyns Rods dealer and pay the tier fee directly for a same-model replacement. Call ahead to verify the dealer participates and has your rod model in stock. Dealers cannot process upgrades. Those must go through Dobyns directly.

**Can I upgrade to a different Dobyns rod when submitting a replacement?**
**Can I upgrade my broken rod to a better series?**
Yes. Under the Warranty Upgrade option, the fee is: (MSRP of new rod) minus (MSRP of broken rod) + (broken rod tier fee), plus return shipping and taxes. No downgrading is available. Upgrades only.
