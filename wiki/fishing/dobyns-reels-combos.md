## Dobyns - Maverick Reels & Combos - Overview

Dobyns produces Maverick Reels in spinning and casting versions, plus Maverick Combos that pair a Maverick rod with a matching Maverick reel. Reels are available in accent colors of green, blue, and red to match Dobyns rod series. Gary Dobyns wanted to create high-performance reels at a budget-minded price point.

Maverick Reels carry a **Limited 1-Year Warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship (original owner, receipt required). See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]] for warranty details. For rod specs on the Maverick rod series, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-product-catalog]].

---

## Dobyns - Maverick Spinning Reel - Specs & Features

The Maverick Spinning Reel is available in 2 sizes: **2000** and **2500**. Available in 3 accent colors: Green, Blue, and Red.

**Features:**
- 6BB+1RB ball bearings
- Gear ratio: 5.2
- New gear structure, precision machined
- Aluminum forged spool

---

## Dobyns - Maverick Casting Reel - Specs & Features

The Maverick Casting Reel is a **100 size** low-profile reel available in **3 gear ratios**: 6.5, 7.2, and 8.1. Available in 3 accent colors: Green, Blue, and Red.

**Features:**
- Low profile design with streamlined shape
- Fully adjustable magnetic cast control
- Titanium coated line guide
- New gear structure, precision machined and low noise

## Dobyns - Maverick Reels - Questions - Specs

**What reels does Dobyns make?**
**Does Dobyns sell reels?**
Yes. Dobyns produces Maverick Spinning Reels (2000 and 2500 sizes, 5.2 gear ratio, 6BB+1RB) and Maverick Casting Reels (100 size, 3 gear ratios: 6.5, 7.2, and 8.1, with magnetic cast control and titanium coated line guide). Both are available in green, blue, and red accent colors.

**What gear ratios are available for the Maverick Casting Reel?**
The Maverick Casting Reel is available in 3 gear ratios: 6.5, 7.2, and 8.1.

---

## Dobyns - Maverick Combos - Questions - What Is Included

**What comes in a Dobyns Maverick Combo?**
**Does Dobyns sell rod and reel combos?**
Yes. Each Maverick Combo pairs a Maverick series rod with a matching Maverick reel. Spinning combos include a Maverick 2500 Blue spinning reel. Casting combos include a Maverick 7.2 Blue casting reel in either right hand (RH) or left hand (LH) retrieve. There are 5 spinning combos and 12 casting combos (6 RH + 6 LH).

**Is the Maverick Combo available in left hand retrieve?**
Yes. All 6 casting combo rod models are available in both right hand (RH) and left hand (LH) retrieve versions.

## Dobyns - Maverick Reels - Questions - Warranty

**What warranty do Dobyns Maverick Reels have?**
**Is the Maverick Reel covered under warranty?**
Dobyns Maverick Reels carry a Limited 1-Year Warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty is limited to the original owner, and a purchase receipt is required. Contact 877-269-8490 or email fishinginfo@gsmorg.com to start a reel warranty claim. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]] for full details.
