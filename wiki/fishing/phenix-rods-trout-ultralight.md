## Phenix Mirage vs Dragonfly — Ultralight Series Differences

**What is the difference between the Phenix Mirage and the Dragonfly?**
**Mirage vs Dragonfly — which should I choose for trout and ultralight fishing?**
Both the Mirage and Dragonfly use Phenix's Net V Advanced Carbon Fiber construction. Key differences:
- **Mirage**: Moderate action only, 1–5 lb line, lure weights 1/32–1/8 oz — the softest, most forgiving rod in the lineup. Best for panfish, crappie, and light trout. Available 5'–8' (1-piece and 2-piece). Tier-2 ($70 replacement fee).
- **Dragonfly**: Net V carbon fiber with both Moderate AND Fast action options, 1–7 lb line, lure weights 1/32–1/4 oz — stiffer and more versatile than the Mirage. Available 8'–9' only. Tier-3 ($95 replacement fee).
Choose **Mirage** for the softest presentation, smallest fish (panfish/crappie), or if you need a rod under 8'. Choose **Dragonfly** if you need more backbone, Fast action option, or heavier lures up to 1/4 oz.

## Phenix Iron Feather, Elixir, Mirage, Dragonfly — Choosing the Right Series

**What is the difference between Iron Feather, Elixir, Mirage, and Dragonfly?**
**Iron Feather**: Premium flagship, extra-fast action, 1–9 lb, solid glass tip, Fuji Titanium SiC guides — for anglers who demand the absolute best ultralight. **Elixir**: Fly-rod-inspired, fast action, 1/4 lb–4–10 lb, widest model range including telescopic — best all-around trout rod. **Mirage**: Net V carbon fiber, Moderate action, 1–5 lb — softest, most forgiving, best for panfish and crappie. **Dragonfly**: Net V carbon fiber, fast/moderate action, 1–7 lb — between Mirage and Elixir in stiffness.

**What is the best Phenix rod for panfish and crappie?**
The Mirage — moderate action, 1–5 lb line, lure weights 1/32–1/8 oz, available down to 5' length. The 2-piece option breaks above the handle for compact storage.

**What is the best Phenix ultralight rod for trout streams?**
The Elixir for versatility (5'6"–9', many line weight options). The Iron Feather for anglers who want the most sensitive premium rod (7'1"–8'6", 1–9 lb). Both come in split grip and full grip configurations.

**Does the Elixir come in a telescopic version?**
Yes — 3 Elixir Telescopic models, all 8', with a telescopic handle section (70/30 split, handle section extends, does not come apart). Line ratings 1–10 lb. Available in Full Grip (FG) and Split Grip (SG).

## Phenix Iron Feather Replacement Cost and Warranty — Tier-5

**How much does it cost to replace an Iron Feather?**
The Iron Feather is Tier-5 — the highest replacement tier. Replacement fee: **$165.00**. Return shipping: $20 (rods 7'10" and under) or $50 (rods 7'11" and over). Total cost: $185–$215 depending on rod length.

**What warranty tier is the Iron Feather?**
Tier-5 ($165 replacement fee). Elixir: Tier-2 ($70). Mirage: Tier-2 ($70). Dragonfly: Tier-3 ($95). See [[fishing/phenix-rods-tier-fees]].

**How do I submit a warranty claim for my Iron Feather?**
Cut the 4"–6" logo section above the reel seat, complete the Warranty Application Form, and mail both with $165 Tier-5 payment to: Fishing Returns, 5250 Frye Rd, Irving TX 75061. See [[fishing/phenix-rods-warranty]] for full procedure.
