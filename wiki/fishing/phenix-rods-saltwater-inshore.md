## Phenix M1 Inshore, RTS Inshore, Titan — Choosing the Right Series

**What is the M1 Inshore best used for?**
Calico bass in thick kelp, mid-summer pelagics, and versatile inshore/nearshore fishing from beach to offshore. Extra fast action with extended foregrip. Available 7'3"–9', 10–45 lb, lure weights 1/2–8 oz, as light as 5.2 oz.

**What is the RTS Inshore designed for?**
Gulf Coast flats fishing specifically — Reds, Trout, and Snook. Lightest, most sensitive inshore rod in the Phenix lineup. Toray T-40 carbon with Nanolite resins, Essex T-Ring FrameLok guides. Available 6'9"–7'7", 6–20 lb, lure weights 1/4–3/4 oz, as light as 4.1 oz.

**What is the difference between M1 Inshore and RTS Inshore?**
The M1 Inshore is for West Coast inshore/nearshore (heavier: 10–45 lb, extra fast action). The RTS is designed for Gulf Coast flats — lighter line (6–20 lb), lighter rods (4.1–5.3 oz), moderate fast action. Choose M1 for kelp/nearshore; choose RTS for flats.

**What is the Black Diamond Inshore best for?**
West Coast inshore finesse — calico bass along the kelp line and breakwall. Built on Black Diamond blanks with Cork/EVA or X-tube handle and Fuji TCS/TDPS trigger reel seat. Available 7'6"–9', 8–40 lb, as light as 8.7 oz.

**What rod should I use for slow pitch jigging?**
The Titan Slow Jigging series — specifically designed for slow pitch and long fall jigging. Zero Gravity balance for all-day fishing with zero fatigue. Jig weights 20–480g. Slow Pitch models 6'3"–6'8"; Long Fall/Stick Bait 7'10".

**What Phenix rod is best for tuna jigging?**
Megalodon Jigging (T12/T40 technology, PE 2–10, jig weights 100–500g) for deep water jigging. Titan Slow Jigging for slow pitch techniques. Megalodon Popping or Titan Popping for surface popping at breaking tuna.
