This page covers the complete Bucca Brand product lineup with model details, SKUs, and color options. Bucca Brand produces ABS hard plastic swimbaits designed by Mike Bucca. All products are available at baits.com and through authorized retailers.

## Bucca Brand — Trick Shad — Overview & Sizes

The Trick Shad is Bucca Brand's best-selling glide bait, designed by Mike Bucca. ABS hard plastic construction allows for a range of colors not found in resin models. All sizes are slow sink and include a replacement tail. The 6" was the original ABS version (based on Mike Bucca's 7" resin Trick Shad); the 4" and 8" are newer size extensions.

## Bucca Brand — Questions — Choosing a Bucca Brand Swimbait

**What Bucca Brand swimbait should I use for bass?**
For freshwater bass, the Trick Shad (4", 6", or 8" glide bait) is the best seller. The Baby Bull Shad (3.75") and Baby Bull Gill (3.75") are multi-jointed options that mimic shad and panfish. For topwater, try the Buzzing Baby Bull Shad or Baby Bull Rat. For fishing around vegetation, the Weedless Baby Bull Shad is designed to be weedless.

**What Bucca Brand swimbait should I use in saltwater?**
The Bull Mullet is Bucca Brand's only saltwater swimbait. Available in 5.5" (1.5 oz) and 8" (3.75 oz) with 3X saltwater grade hooks and heavy-duty rotating hook hangers.
