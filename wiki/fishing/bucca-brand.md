## Bucca Brand — Overview

Bucca Brand is a fishing lure brand under GSM Outdoors, created in collaboration with renowned self-taught swimbait designer Mike Bucca. The brand is a collaboration 20 years in the making — an intertwining of journeys and visions with one goal: bring the creativity of a master bait designer to life in a value form to enhance the fishing experience for all anglers. Countless pro anglers have used Mike Bucca's swimbaits, and Bucca Brand takes pride in providing affordable swimbaits for the masses.

All Bucca Brand hard swimbaits feature ABS plastic construction, which allows for diverse, lifelike color patterns not possible with traditional resin-poured models. Products are available at baits.com and through authorized retailers.

## Bucca Brand — Freshwater Product Lines

Bucca Brand offers multiple freshwater swimbait families, all designed by Mike Bucca with ABS plastic construction:

- **Trick Shad** — Hard plastic glide bait (4", 6", 8"). Slow sink. Mustad Triple Grip hooks, replaceable tail, swivel hook hangers.
- **BFS Trick Shad** — Compact 2.5" glide bait
- **Baby Bull Shad** — 3.75" multi-jointed swimbait (4 segments, 1/2 oz). Brushed tail.
- **Lil Baby Bull Shad** — Downsized 2.5" multi-jointed swimbait
- **5" Bull Shad** — Mid-size multi-jointed swimbait with brush tail
- **Baby Bull Gill** — 3.75" multi-jointed gill-profile swimbait (4 segments, 3/4 oz, slow sinking). Creates "S" motion.
- **Buzzing Baby Bull Shad** — Baby Bull Shad body with inline buzzbait arm (topwater, 1/2 oz)
- **Weedless Baby Bull Shad** — 4.5" weedless swimbait with weighted EWG hook (3/4 oz)
- **Baby Bull Rat** — 3.5" jointed topwater rat (2 segments, 3/4 oz, depth 0–1')
- **5" Bull Wake** — Jointed wake bait with flexible tail. Mustad Triple Grip hooks.

For detailed specs, SKUs, and colors, see [[fishing/bucca-brand-product-catalog]].

## Bucca Brand — Saltwater Product Line

The **Bull Mullet** is Bucca Brand's saltwater swimbait, designed by Mike Bucca. It is a multi-jointed swimbait (4 segments) built to mimic fleeing baitfish in coastal waters. Available in 5.5" (1.5 oz) and 8" (3.75 oz) sizes. Features 3X saltwater grade hooks and heavy-duty rotating hook hangers. Available in 7 saltwater baitfish colors per size. Best retrieved quickly under the surface.

## Bucca Brand — Warranty & Returns — Broken or Defective Product

**My Bucca Brand swimbait is broken. What do I do?**
**My Trick Shad is defective. How do I get a replacement?**
**My Bucca Brand product stopped working. What is the process?**
If your Bucca Brand product has a manufacturing defect, submit a warranty replacement request at baits.com/warranty-replacement-request/. You will need: proof of purchase (original sales receipt), photos of the defective product, and your address. GSM Outdoors will review the claim and, if approved, issue a Return Authorization (RA) number with return instructions. Do NOT ship the product before receiving an RA number — unauthorized shipments will be refused. Allow up to 21 days to receive your replacement after your return is processed. There is no mailing address to send product to without first obtaining an RA number through the online form.

**What is the warranty on Bucca Brand products?**
**Does Bucca Brand have a warranty?**
Yes. All Bucca Brand products are covered by the GSM Outdoors warranty for manufacturing defects. The warranty is valid for the original owner who purchased from a GSM Outdoors authorized retailer. Submit claims at baits.com/warranty-replacement-request/

**How do I return a Bucca Brand product for a refund?**
**How do I file a warranty claim for Bucca Brand?**
Returns for refund: go to baits.com/return-request/ within 30 days of purchase. Product must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging. Warranty replacements for defects: go to baits.com/warranty-replacement-request/ In both cases, wait for a Return Authorization (RA) number before shipping anything.
