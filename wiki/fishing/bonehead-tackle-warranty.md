## Bonehead Tackle Warranty — Overview

BoneHead Tackle offers warranty coverage for both standard Carbon Fiber Rods and E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods. Warranty duration and replacement policies differ between the product lines. Customers commonly ask about warranty length, replacement costs, proof of purchase requirements, and coverage exclusions.

---

## E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods — Warranty

**What is the warranty on BoneHead E-Series rods?**
**How long is the BoneHead E-Series warranty?**
BoneHead E-Series Carbon Fiber Rods include a limited 90-day warranty covering manufacturing defects and workmanship issues. The warranty lasts 90 days from the original purchase date.

**Does the BoneHead E-Series warranty cover accidental damage?**
No. The E-Series warranty does not cover misuse, neglect, or accidental damage.

**Do I need a receipt for a BoneHead E-Series warranty claim?**
Yes. Proof of purchase such as the original receipt is required for warranty claims.

---

## Carbon Fiber Rods — Warranty

**What is the warranty on BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods?**
**How long is the BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rod warranty?**
BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods include a limited 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and workmanship issues. The warranty lasts 1 year from the original purchase date.

**Is there a fee if I need a replacement under warranty?**
**What is the replacement fee for BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods?**
BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rod warranty replacements are subject to a flat-rate $30 replacement fee.

**Does the BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rod warranty cover accidental damage?**
No. The warranty does not cover neglect, misuse, or accidental damage.

## Bonehead Tackle Warranty — Coverage Summary

**How long is the warranty on your rods?**
**Is the E-Series warranty the same as the premium rod warranty?**
No. The E-Series carries a limited 90-day warranty. The premium Carbon Fiber Rods carry a limited 1-year warranty. The two warranties are different in duration and cost.

**Do you offer a lifetime warranty?**
No. Bonehead Tackle does not offer a lifetime warranty. The maximum warranty coverage is 1 year for Carbon Fiber Rods and 90 days for E-Series rods.

**What is the longest warranty Bonehead Tackle offers?**
The longest warranty is 1 year, available on the premium Carbon Fiber Rods. E-Series rods carry a 90-day warranty.

**What does the BoneHead warranty cover?**
The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.

**What is not covered under the BoneHead warranty?**
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, corrosion, accidental damage, misuse, modification, or improper storage.

## Bonehead Tackle Warranty — Claims Process

**How do I file a BoneHead warranty claim?**
Keep your proof of purchase, contact BoneHead Tackle support, describe the issue, and provide photos if requested. Contact support before shipping any product for replacement or inspection.

**Do I need my receipt to make a warranty claim?**
Yes. Proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims.

**Where do I contact BoneHead Tackle for warranty support?**
Customers can contact BoneHead Tackle through the official support page before shipping any product for replacement or inspection.
