## Dobyns - Lures & Terminal Tackle - Overview

Dobyns produces a line of spinnerbaits, football jigs, swimbait heads, football heads, and nail weights. All jigs and spinnerbaits are hand-tied with custom colored skirts. This page covers product specs, colors, sizes, and features for each product line.

For rods, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-product-catalog]]. For reels and combos, see [[fishing/dobyns-reels-combos]]. For the brand overview, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods]].

## Dobyns - D-Blade Advantage Spinnerbait - Specs & Features

The D-Blade Advantage is the standard Dobyns spinnerbait featuring custom head designs and colors. The skirts are hand-tied so strands and colors stay in the proper place, allowing the angler to slap the bait on the water to free moss or grass without skirt movement.

**Specs:** 2 sizes, 7 colors. Colorado/Willow and Willow/Willow blade combinations.

**Features:**
- Hand-tied silicone skirt
- Custom Mustad hook
- Nickel or gold plated custom blades
- Ball bearing swivel
- 0.040 high grade stainless steel wire
- Special bait keepers for trailer
- Unique head design with custom head colors

## Dobyns - D-Blade Beast Spinnerbait - Specs & Features

The D-Blade Beast is the premium Dobyns spinnerbait. The silicone skirt strands are singly placed and tied one by one around the head for a perfectly even and balanced look. The unique head design prevents the skirt from balling up.

**Specs:** 3 sizes, 9 colors. Colorado/Willow and Willow/Willow blade combinations.

**Features:**
- Silicone skirt strands singly placed for even, perfectly balanced look
- Custom Mustad hook with longer shank
- Premium stainless steel nickel or gold plated blades
- High grade ball bearing swivel
- Welded eye
- 0.040 high grade stainless steel wire
- Special bait keepers to hold trailer

## Dobyns - D-Nail Weights - Specs & Features

The Dobyns Nail Weights have a very sharp point to insert and rig the bait straight. The shorter nail allows the bait to have more overall action. A small ball head makes insertion easy. Multiple ridges on the nail prevent it from falling out.

**Specs:** Available in 3 sizes.

## Dobyns - Lures - Questions - Spinnerbaits

**What spinnerbaits does Dobyns make?**
**What is the difference between D-Blade Advantage and D-Blade Beast?**
Dobyns makes two spinnerbait lines: the D-Blade Advantage (2 sizes, 7 colors) and the D-Blade Beast (3 sizes, 9 colors). Both are hand-tied with silicone skirts, custom Mustad hooks, and ball bearing swivels. The Beast is the premium version with a welded eye, longer shank Mustad hook, and individually placed skirt strands for a perfectly balanced look. The Advantage features a unique head design and is the standard offering.

## Dobyns - Lures - Questions - Jigs

**What jigs does Dobyns make?**
**What is the difference between Dobyns Extreme Football Jig and Football Jig?**
Dobyns makes three jig lines: the Extreme Football Jig (Gamakatsu 5/0 EWG hook, 3 sizes, 12 colors), the Football Jig (Gamakatsu 604 round bend hook, 5 sizes, 12 colors), and the Extreme Jig Spotted Bass Special (Gamakatsu 4/0 EWG hook, 6 colors). The main difference between the Extreme and standard Football Jig is the hook: the Extreme uses an EWG jig hook while the standard uses Gary Dobyns' preferred 604 round bend hook.

**Does Dobyns make a jig for spotted bass or smallmouth?**
Yes. The Extreme Jig Spotted Bass Special uses a smaller Gamakatsu 4/0 EWG hook designed for spotted bass and smallmouth with their smaller mouths. Available in 6 colors.

## Dobyns - Lures - Questions - Terminal Tackle

**Does Dobyns make swimbait heads?**
Yes. The D-Swim Bait Head features a 4-barbed bait keeper that holds swimbait bodies without super glue. Available in 6 sizes, 3 colors (Ayu, Shad, Baby Bass), and 3 hook types (regular, heavy wire, heavy wire with weed guard). Sold in packs of 3.

**Does Dobyns make football heads?**
Yes. Dobyns Football Heads use the Gamakatsu 604 hook with a 4-point bait keeper and recessed eye. Available with or without weed guard, in 3 colors (Black, Brown, Green Pumpkin) and 5 sizes (1/4 oz through 1 oz). Made with the Yamamoto Hula Grubs in mind.
