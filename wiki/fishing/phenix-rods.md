## Phenix Rods Products

## Phenix Rods — Brand Overview

Phenix Rods ("Phenix") is a custom quality rod brand under GSM Outdoors with nearly five decades of history. Phenix has set the standard for saltwater and pelagic fishing rods and created the industry's first fully woven carbon fiber fishing rod (the Black Diamond Hybrid). The brand covers a complete range of fishing applications — saltwater, freshwater bass, trout and ultralight, salmon and steelhead, travel, and specialty jigging/popping — along with braided fishing lines and accessories.

Phenix utilizes advanced materials including Toray carbon fiber (T-12 through T-46 ton), proprietary Nanolite resins, S-Glass composites, and woven carbon fiber/Kevlar blends. Rods feature premium components from Fuji, Essex, Alps, and Sea Guide.

## Phenix Rods — Product Categories

Phenix rods are organized into four main categories:

- **Saltwater** — Abyss, Abyss HD, Axis, Black Diamond, Black Diamond Hybrid, Black Diamond East Coast, Black Diamond Inshore, Black Diamond Surf, Pandora, M1 Inshore, RTS Inshore, Titan Slow Jigging, Titan Popping, Megalodon Jigging, Megalodon Popping
- **Freshwater** — Classic BFS, Kokanee Reaper, M1 Walleye, K2, M1, Ultra MBX Classic, Ultra Swimbait, Ultra Swimbait Classic, Maxim, Feather, Recon Elite, Crankbait XG (Glass), Crankbait Composite X, Cicada, Trifecta Pro, Trifecta Lite, Trifecta, Super Flipper, Black Chrome
- **Trout & Ultralight** — Iron Feather, Elixir (including Telescopic), Mirage, Dragonfly
- **Travel** — Redeye Saltwater, Redeye Freshwater (Bass), Redeye Trout/Fly

Additional products include **braided fishing lines** (Hydra 8X, Feather 16X Hollow, IronFeather 8X, Trout Braid) and **accessories** (reel covers, bags, coolers, pliers, line cutters, measuring boards).

## Phenix Rods — Saltwater Rod Series

## Phenix Abyss — Overview
Designed for West Coast style surface jig and live bait anglers. Constructed with Toray M40J Graphite/S-Glass composites, the Abyss provides an ideal strength-to-weight balance with superior lifting power while remaining lightweight to reduce casting fatigue.

## Phenix Abyss HD — Overview
An upgraded version of the Abyss with a composite blank blending S-Glass and Mitsubishi Core Carbon Fiber for strength, sensitivity, and weight reduction. Features a Phenix exclusive "Custom Grip" non-slip corded rubber grip available in three colors (Black, Orange, Red).

## Phenix Axis — Overview
Built with composite blends of S-Glass, T-16 carbon fiber, and graphite materials with multi-helix construction. The Axis is designed for any saltwater environment — dynamic in performance, quality, and sensitivity with backbone structure for total confidence. Dark blue rod blank color.

## Phenix Black Diamond — Overview
Constructed with Toray's high-strength carbon fibers using a unique layering process and proprietary resin. The Black Diamond has a thinner diameter and lower weight — the lightest rod in its class.

## Phenix Black Diamond Hybrid — Overview
The world's first fully woven carbon fiber fishing rod blank. A unique blend of high-strength woven carbon fiber and Kevlar — the latest technology from aerospace and automotive industries applied to fishing. Lightweight yet unbelievably strong, with action far superior to traditional glass or graphite.

## Phenix Black Diamond East Coast — Overview
Utilizes the same blank construction as the Black Diamond series but designed for East Coast pelagic species. Features Toray T-40 NanoTube carbon fiber with Phenix proprietary Nanolite resins.

## Phenix Black Diamond Inshore — Overview
Built with Black Diamond blanks, specifically designed for inshore fishing — finesse fishing the breakwall or tight along the kelp line. Unique Cork/EVA or X-tube handle paired with Fuji TCS or TDPS trigger reel seat.

## Phenix Black Diamond Surf — Overview
Designed for surf casters. Ultra-sensitive and lightweight with superior performance. Manufactured with 30 and 40 ton Toray composite material.

## Phenix Pandora — Overview
A high-end saltwater series designed for female anglers. Uses the same technologies as the Black Diamond and Axis series but with a custom Phenix-designed handle blending EVA and proprietary Sure-Grip non-slip handle system. Pink anodized Essex T-Ring premium carbide guides with FrameLok technology.

## Phenix M1 Inshore — Overview
Ideal for fishing calico bass in thick kelp or mid-summer pelagics. Lightweight yet powerful. Actions vary from fast to extra fast with an extended foregrip handle.

## Phenix RTS Inshore — Overview
Designed by Gulf Coast fishermen and guides. Super light Toray T-40 ton carbon fiber blank with Phenix Nanolite resins. The lightest, most sensitive inshore rod on the market.

## Phenix Titan Slow Jigging — Overview
Designed for slow pitch and long fall jigging techniques. Features Zero Gravity technology for balance and zero fatigue. Acid-wrapped Fuji K-series SiC guides to alleviate torque when fighting big fish from the depths.

## Phenix Titan Popping — Overview
Built from Toray multi-composite materials. 2-piece design joined above the foregrip for optimal travel. Designed for up to 15KG max drag. Suitable for breaking tuna or crashing GT's.

## Phenix Megalodon Jigging — Overview
Crafted with T12 and T40 technology for unique strength and lifting power to handle tuna, amberjack, GT's, and other powerful species.

## Phenix Rods — Questions — Comparing Series

**What is the best Phenix rod for tuna jigging?**
For slow pitch / long fall jigging: Titan Slow Jigging (Zero Gravity balance system, Fuji K-series SiC guides, up to 130 lb). For surface popping on tuna and GT: Titan Popping or Megalodon Popping (up to 15KG drag). For high-speed jigging targeting tuna, amberjack, and GT at depth: Megalodon Jigging (T12/T40 construction, purpose-built lifting power). For lighter inshore or kelp-zone tuna with live bait: Abyss or Abyss HD (15–50 lb, West Coast style). Match the jigging style to the series.

**What is the difference between the Abyss and the Abyss HD?**
The Abyss uses Toray M40J Graphite/S-Glass with Essex SiC guides and cork tape handle. The Abyss HD upgrades to S-Glass/Mitsubishi Core Carbon Fiber, Fuji FazLite guides, and Phenix's exclusive non-slip rubber Custom Grip in Black, Orange, or Red. The HD is lighter, more sensitive, and has a better grip.

**What is the difference between the Feather and the M1?**
Both are premium bass rods. The Feather uses a cross-weave of 36 and 40-ton carbon fiber with the balance point in the palm — optimized for light finesse presentations. The M1 uses 36-ton Toray with K-Woven scrim and a Vortex one-piece blank — optimized for sensitivity and tournament fishing across a wider lure weight range.

**What is the difference between Cicada and Trifecta for salmon?**
The Cicada is Phenix's lightest, most sensitive salmon rod (30-ton Toray, carbon fiber rear grip). The Trifecta is the original proven workhorse (40-ton composite) at a lower price. For most anglers the Trifecta is sufficient; serious guides and tournament anglers prefer the Cicada.

**Which Phenix rod series is best for beginners?**
For saltwater: Abyss (proven, versatile). For freshwater bass: Maxim or Recon Elite (best value). For trout: Elixir (widest model range, multiple length/action options). For salmon: Trifecta (most affordable).

## Phenix Rods — Questions — Warranty & Support

**My Phenix rod broke — what do I do?**
Cut the 4"–6" logo section above the reel seat, complete the Warranty Application Form, and mail both with payment to: Fishing Returns, 5250 Frye Rd, Irving TX 75061. Full steps at [[fishing/phenix-rods-warranty]].

**How much does a Phenix warranty replacement cost?**
Tier 1 (Feather, Maxim, Crankbait XG): $55. Up to Tier 5 (Iron Feather, K2, Black Diamond Surf): $165. Plus return shipping ($20 for rods 7'10" and under, $50 for over 7'11"). Full table at [[fishing/phenix-rods-tier-fees]].

**Can I upgrade my rod when I send it in for warranty?**
Yes. Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of new rod) − (MSRP of broken rod) + (tier fee of broken rod). No downgrading allowed.
