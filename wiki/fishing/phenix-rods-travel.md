## Phenix RedEye Travel — Choosing the Right Series

**What makes the RedEye travel rods different from other travel rods?**
Most travel rods have flat spots or power/action changes at the ferrule connections. Phenix engineered the RedEye series to bend uniformly like a one-piece blank regardless of the number of pieces. Every rod comes with a felt-lined hard case under 36 inches.

**Which RedEye series should I choose for my trip?**
**Offshore/nearshore saltwater**: RedEye Saltwater (3-piece, 7'–9'2", 12–100 lb). **Bass fishing or tropical inshore**: RedEye Freshwater/Bass (3-piece, 7'–8', 6–40 lb, extra fast). **Trout, crappie, or finesse bass**: RedEye Trout/Fly (3-piece, 6'–8', 1–6 lb).

**Can I take a RedEye rod on a plane?**
Yes — all RedEye rods break into 3 or 4 pieces with a hard case under 36 inches, designed specifically for airline travel (overhead compartments and checked luggage).
