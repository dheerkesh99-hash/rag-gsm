## Dobyns Rods - Tier Fees and Costs - Overview

This page lists all Dobyns Rods No-Hassle Replacement tier fees, return shipping fees, sales tax states, and the warranty upgrade fee formula. All fees listed here apply to the No-Hassle Replacement Program and the Warranty Upgrade Program.

For the step-by-step replacement process, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]]. For warranty coverage and eligibility, see [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]].

**Quick reference — total cost to replace (tier fee + $30 shipping):**
- Colt: $30 + $30 = **$60 total**
- Maverick: $40 + $30 = **$70 total**
- Fury: $50 + $30 = **$80 total**
- Kaden / Sierra / Sierra Micro / Sierra Ultra Finesse / Sierra Trout & Panfish: $60 + $30 = **$90 total**
- Champion XP / Mike Bucca BullShad / Josh Jones Hyperlite: $70 + $30 = **$100 total**
- Champion Extreme HP / Kaden Travel: $80 + $30 = **$110 total**
- Xtasy: $125 + $30 = **$155 total**

## Dobyns Rods - Warranty Upgrade Fee Formula

To upgrade a broken rod to a higher series, the fee is:

**Upgrade Fee = (MSRP of upgrade rod) minus (MSRP of broken rod) + (Broken rod tier fee)**

Return shipping fees and applicable sales taxes also apply.

**Example:** Upgrade a Fury 734C to a Champion Extreme 744C SH. $369.99 (DX MSRP) minus $149.99 (FR MSRP) + $50.00 (FR Tier Fee) = **$270.00 Upgrade Fee**. Plus $30.00 return shipping and applicable sales tax.

No downgrade option is available. You may only upgrade to a rod in a higher series. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-replacement]] for the full upgrade submission process.

## Dobyns Rods - Tier Fees - Questions - General

**Do I have to pay for a Dobyns rod replacement?**
**Is the Dobyns No-Hassle Replacement free?**
No. The No-Hassle Replacement Program requires payment of a tier fee (ranging from $30.00 to $125.00 depending on the rod series), a $30.00 domestic return shipping fee, and applicable sales tax. The only free replacement is for confirmed manufacturing defects within 60 days of purchase. See [[fishing/dobyns-rods-warranty]] for the defect claim process.

**Do I have to pay sales tax on my Dobyns replacement?**
Sales tax applies when shipping to: AR, AZ, CA, GA, TX, ID, IA, LA, MO, MS, OH, UT, KY, MN, NY, TN, WA, and WI. If paying by check or money order, include the tax. If unsure, use the Pay by Phone option at 877-269-8490.
