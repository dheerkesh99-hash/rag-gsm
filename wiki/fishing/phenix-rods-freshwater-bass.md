## Phenix M1 Bass, Feather, K2, Ultra Swimbait — Choosing the Right Series

**What is the best Phenix rod for tournament bass fishing?**
The M1 or Feather. The M1 uses 36-ton Toray with K-Woven scrim and Vortex one-piece blank — proven tournament standard. The Feather uses cross-weave 36/40-ton carbon with balance point in the palm — best for light finesse presentations. For heavy cover and heavier lures, choose M1. For finesse, choose Feather.

**What is the best Phenix rod for big swimbait fishing?**
The Ultra Swimbait Classic (flagship, handles up to 8 oz, woven carbon/Kevlar Hybrid blank) or Ultra Swimbait (same lure range, slightly heavier rod). Both are casting only, 7'9"–8'.

**What is the difference between M1 and K2?**
Both are premium bass rods. The M1 uses 36-ton Toray with K-Woven scrim. The K2 uses Toray T-46 ton — the highest available composite — with Fuji Titanium Torzite guides. The K2 is the more premium rod with maximum sensitivity. The M1 is the proven tournament standard.

**What is the Classic BFS designed for?**
Bait Finesse System fishing — heavily pressured waters, ultra-light line (4–10 lb), and micro lures. 36-ton Toray with solid carbon tip and Fuji Alconite micro guides for maximum feel with finesse tactics.

**What is the difference between Ultra Swimbait and Ultra Swimbait Classic?**
The Ultra Swimbait Classic is built on Phenix's Hybrid Technology blank (woven carbon fiber/Kevlar) — lighter, faster, and more sensitive. It's the flagship big bait rod. The Ultra Swimbait uses 40-ton Toray carbon. Both handle lures up to 8 oz.

## Phenix Feather (Bass Rod) Replacement Cost and Warranty — Tier-1

**How much does it cost to replace a Phenix Feather rod?**
**What is the replacement fee for the Feather bass rod?**
The Phenix Feather (freshwater bass rod) is Tier-1 — the lowest replacement tier. Replacement fee: **$55.00**. Return shipping: $20 (rods 7'10" and under) or $50 (rods 7'11" and over). Total cost: **$75–$105** depending on rod length. Note: this is the Feather bass rod (FTX series), not the Iron Feather trout/ultralight rod (which is Tier-5 at $165).

**What warranty tier is the Phenix Feather (bass rod)?**
The Feather (bass rod) is Tier-1 ($55 replacement fee). M1 Bass: Tier-2 ($70). Ultra MBX Classic: Tier-3 ($95). K2: Tier-5 ($165). Iron Feather (trout rod): Tier-5 ($165). See [[fishing/phenix-rods-tier-fees]].

**How do I submit a warranty claim for my Phenix Feather rod?**
Cut the 4"–6" logo section above the reel seat, complete the Warranty Application Form, and mail both with the $55 Tier-1 payment to: Fishing Returns, 5250 Frye Rd, Irving TX 75061. See [[fishing/phenix-rods-warranty]] for full procedure.
