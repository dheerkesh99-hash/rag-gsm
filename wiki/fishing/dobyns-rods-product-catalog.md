## Dobyns Rods - Xtasy (DRX) - Overview

Ultra-high-end "feel" series. Super light, very sensitive, incredibly balanced with fast crisp actions. Made for jigs, worms, and drop-shot styles of fishing. Excels with deep water techniques where super sensitivity is required. Each rod has a unique serial number. 15 models (5 spinning, 10 casting). Tier fee: $125.00. Limited Lifetime Warranty.

**Features:** Toray Nano graphite blanks, Fuji titanium Torzite guides, painted Fuji graphite reel seats, all custom cosmetics and handle, Kevlar wrapping, full handle AAAA-grade Portugal cork grips.
