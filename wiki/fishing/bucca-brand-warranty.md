## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — Overview

This page covers the GSM Outdoors warranty replacement policy as it applies to Bucca Brand products. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and is valid only for the original owner who purchased from an authorized retailer.

This page explains what is covered, the eligibility requirements, what to expect after submitting a claim, and important shipping rules. For standard returns and refunds (non-warranty), see [[fishing/bucca-brand-returns]]. For product details and SKUs, see [[fishing/bucca-brand-product-catalog]].

---

## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — What Is Covered

All GSM Outdoors products, including Bucca Brand, have a warranty that covers manufacturing defects. GSM Outdoors states: "we stand proudly behind every item bearing our name." The warranty is valid only for the original owner of the product purchased from an authorized retailer. Specific warranty time frames vary by product — refer to your individual product documentation for the applicable duration.

---

## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — Eligibility Requirements

For a Bucca Brand warranty claim to be approved, all of the following conditions must be met:

1. You must have purchased the product from a **GSM Outdoors authorized retailer**
2. You must provide an **original sales receipt** (proof of purchase)
3. You must have used the product **only as intended** and in accordance with the product care instructions — tampered with and/or altered units will void the warranty
4. **Photos of the defective product** must be provided with your claim submission

All four conditions are mandatory. If any condition is not met, the warranty claim will not be approved.

---

## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — After You Submit a Claim

After you submit a warranty replacement request, GSM Outdoors will review your claim. If the claim meets all warranty criteria, GSM will provide a **Return Authorization (RA) number** along with instructions on how to return your product.

If the original item is no longer available, GSM Outdoors will send a **comparable replacement product** at their discretion.

**Timeline:** It can take up to **21 days** to receive your replacement after your claim is approved and the product is returned.

---

## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — Shipping Rules

**Return Authorization (RA) required:** Any product received by GSM Outdoors without a Return Authorization will be **refused**, and GSM will not be held responsible. Do not ship your product until you have received an RA number and return instructions.

**Do not include extras:** GSM Outdoors is not responsible for any items received that are not part of the warrantied item. Do not include any accessories or other items in your shipment — send only the defective product covered by the warranty claim.

---

## Bucca Brand — Warranty Replacement — Step-by-Step Process

How to submit a Bucca Brand warranty replacement claim:

1. Go to the GSM Outdoors warranty replacement request page at baits.com/warranty-replacement-request/
2. Fill out the form: select your place of purchase, provide your address, upload photos of the defective product, and describe the warranty issue
3. Submit the form and wait for GSM Outdoors to review your claim
4. If approved, you will receive a **Return Authorization (RA) number** and shipping instructions
5. Ship **only** the defective product (no accessories or extra items) to the address provided, following the RA instructions
6. Once GSM receives and processes the return, a replacement will be sent — allow up to **21 days** to receive it

Do **not** ship any product before receiving an RA number. Unauthorized shipments will be refused.
