## Dobyns Rods - Brand Overview

Dobyns Rods is the brainchild of tournament angler Gary Dobyns. With decades of tournament angling and over 100 major tournament victories, Gary combined his vast experience and unending pursuit of perfection into Dobyns Rods. The brand offers a full lineup of fishing rods across 16 series, from the affordable Colt to the super-refined Xtasy. Dobyns also produces Maverick Reels, rod-and-reel combos, D-Blade spinnerbaits, football jigs, swimbait heads, and terminal tackle. Dobyns Rods is a GSM Outdoors brand.

Products are available at dobynsrods.com and through authorized Dobyns retailers.

## Dobyns Rods - Premium Rod Series

- **Xtasy (DRX)** - Ultra-high-end series. Toray Nano graphite blanks, Fuji titanium Torzite guides, AAAA-grade Portugal cork, Kevlar wrapping. Unique serial number on each rod. 14 models (5 spinning, 9 casting). Best for jigs, worms, and deep water feel techniques.
- **Champion Extreme HP (DX)** - The ultimate in sensitivity, strength, weight, and balance. Toray high modulus graphite blanks, Fuji guides, Kevlar wrapping, AAA-grade Portugal cork. 28 models (spinning, casting, pitch and flip).

## Dobyns Rods - Maverick Reels & Combos Summary
