## Carbon Fiber Rods — Overview

BoneHead Tackle Carbon Fiber Rods are premium fishing rods built with high-grade carbon fiber for a lightweight but strong performance profile. The series includes multiple spinning rod lengths with stainless steel guides and split cork grips. These rods are designed for anglers looking for strong backbone performance without excess weight.

## Carbon Fiber Rods — Specs & Features

**What features do BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods have?**
BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods use high-grade carbon fiber, stainless steel guides, and split cork grips.

**What kind of guides do BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods use?**
Stainless steel guides.

**Do all Bonehead Tackle rods use stainless steel guides?**
Yes. Both the premium Carbon Fiber rods and the E-Series Carbon Fiber rods use stainless steel guides.

**What grip do BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods have?**
Split cork grips.

**Are BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods two-piece rods?**
The 7' and 8' Neon Green Carbon Fiber Spinning Rods use a 2-piece rod design.

**What colors do BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods come in?**
**Do the premium BoneHead Carbon Fiber rods come in any colors?**
The 10', 8', and 7' Carbon Fiber Spinning Rods are Neon Green. The 5'8" model does not have a listed color variant.

**What sizes are available for BoneHead Carbon Fiber Rods?**
Available sizes include 10', 8', 7', and 5'8" models.

**What is the shortest BoneHead Carbon Fiber rod?**
The shortest premium Carbon Fiber rod is the 5'8" model.

**How light is the 5'8" BoneHead Carbon Fiber Spinning Rod?**
The 5'8" Carbon Fiber Spinning Rod weighs approximately 3.2 ounces.

**Do you make a 12 foot Carbon Fiber rod?**
**Can I buy a 12 foot premium Carbon Fiber rod?**
No. The premium Carbon Fiber lineup only goes up to 10'. If you need a 12 foot rod, the E-Series Carbon Fiber Rod is available in a 12 foot model for $79.99. See [[fishing/bonehead-tackle-e-series-carbon-fiber-spinning-rods]].

**What are the main differences between the Carbon Fiber and E-Series rods?**
**What is the difference between the premium rods and the E-Series?**
The key differences are: (1) **Price** — Carbon Fiber rods cost $109.99–$119.99; E-Series rods cost $59.99–$79.99. (2) **Grip** — Carbon Fiber uses split cork grips; E-Series uses solid EVA grips. (3) **Warranty** — Carbon Fiber carries a 1-year limited warranty with a $30 replacement fee; E-Series carries a 90-day limited warranty. (4) **Available sizes** — E-Series includes a 12' model; the premium Carbon Fiber lineup goes up to 10' only. Both series use carbon fiber construction and stainless steel guides.
