## Phenix Abyss, Abyss HD, Black Diamond, Axis, Pandora — Choosing the Right Series

**What is the best Phenix rod for West Coast live bait fishing?**
The Abyss and Abyss HD are specifically designed for West Coast live bait and surface jig fishing. The Abyss HD adds better sensitivity (Mitsubishi Core Carbon Fiber) and a non-slip rubber grip. Both available 7'2"–9', 10–50 lb.

**What is the difference between the Abyss and Abyss HD?**
The Abyss uses Toray M40J Graphite/S-Glass with Essex SiC guides and cork tape handle. The Abyss HD upgrades to S-Glass/Mitsubishi Core Carbon Fiber, Fuji FazLite guides (double wrapped), and Phenix's exclusive rubber Custom Grip in Black, Orange, or Red.

**What is the Black Diamond Hybrid best used for?**
Heavy saltwater applications requiring maximum strength at minimum weight. First fully woven carbon fiber/Kevlar blank ever made. Handles 15–200 lb line. Best for stand-up fishing, trolling, and chunking for tuna and large pelagics.

**Which Axis model handles 80–200 lb line?**
The Axis X4H models — PHX-HAX-C720X4H (7'2") and PHX-HAX-C760X4H (7'6") for rail rods. Fuji HB SiN guides, Alps/Hypalon grips, 34–38 oz.

**What is the Black Diamond East Coast designed for?**
East Coast and offshore pelagic species (tuna, wahoo, GT). Uses Toray T-40 NanoTube carbon fiber — same blank construction as the West Coast Black Diamond but with high-density EVA grip and Essex T-Ring carbide FrameLok guides. Available 6'6"–7'6".

**Is the Pandora only for female anglers?**
It's designed with female anglers in mind — lighter, with a custom EVA/Sure-Grip handle and pink anodized Essex T-Ring guides — but any angler can use it. Same blank technology as the Black Diamond and Axis. Available 7'–8', 15–60 lb.

## Phenix Black Diamond vs Black Diamond East Coast — Comparison

**What is the difference between the Black Diamond and the Black Diamond East Coast?**
Both the original Black Diamond and the Black Diamond East Coast share the same Toray high-strength carbon fiber blank construction. The key differences are: (1) **Handle** — the original Black Diamond has a cork tape handle; the East Coast version has a high-density EVA grip. (2) **Guides** — the East Coast uses Essex T-Ring carbide FrameLok guides. (3) **Target region and species** — the original Black Diamond is designed for West Coast pelagic fishing (tuna, yellowtail, calico bass); the East Coast version is designed for East Coast offshore species (tuna, wahoo, GT). Both are Tier-3 ($95 replacement fee).

**Do the Black Diamond and Black Diamond East Coast use the same blank?**
Yes. Both are built on the same Toray high-strength carbon fiber blank construction. The differences between the two series are in the handle (cork tape vs high-density EVA) and the guides (East Coast adds Essex T-Ring carbide FrameLok), not the blank material.
